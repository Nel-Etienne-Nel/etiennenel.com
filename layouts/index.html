I’m Etienne. Most of how I think comes down to one question: why? Why does this work, and what’s underneath it? I can’t leave a system alone until I can see the whole of it in my head.

That question is how I ended up in AI. I work with it every day: building chatbots, automations and agents for clients, and teaching people who don’t code how it actually works. I believe in it. But I’ve noticed it doing something to my thinking that I don’t like.

So I split what I write in two. The technical docs explain how things work and why, from things I’ve actually built. I write those with AI. The editorial pieces I write by hand. No polish, no assistance, no safety net. Just an idea and whatever I can make of it. Some of it will be rough. That’s the point.
