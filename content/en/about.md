This blog is an experiment in reclaiming something I think we're all quietly losing — the ability to think a thought all the way through. I work in AI daily; I build with it, I teach it, and I genuinely believe in it. But I've noticed it doing something to my thinking that I don't like. So once a week I sit down and write without AI doing it for me. No polish, no assistance, no safety net. Just an idea, an argument, and whatever I can make of it. Some of it will be rough. That's the point.
