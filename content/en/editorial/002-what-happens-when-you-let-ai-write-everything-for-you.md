AI is great, the day to day benefits can not be disputed. 
When you're using it the right way.

It works well when you need to gather information or put different sources together to build a report. The nature of what makes a LLM so useful is the Language, the fact that it understand languege so well is why its so popular today. 

These advantages do come with a risk however.

As we continue to use it in our day to day lives, in more aspects we grow more dependent as a result. You start out by gathering information and getting all of your sources onto one page. It doesnt write in your natural way and you are fine with that, you just needed to get out the report. 

The next day comes and you need to do the same thing, you repeat the process even faster and think "Wow, this is great, i feel so productive". But do you really know what that report says? Sure a super smart LLM wrote it so it must be right, right? 
AI writes super well, but every word is a statistical assumption made. AI guesses the next word on what it think should be correct. So yes, this report might look amaxing, but when you drill down into the information, what use is it really. Im not saying everything the AI outputs is wrong, im jyst saying you need to be weary of what it says and fact check aggressively.

Moving on,


While going on with your work in weeks and months, AI now writes all of your documents, feedback and emails. By now you have tuned or its learned your tone of voice a little better, hopefully. But it still looks Artificial in most cases...

You send and recieve emails that are much longer than they need to be because AI loves over explaining a point. Because you got so lazy with using AI for everything, you don't read the email and have AI read it for you. Or you feed in a AI generated report into AI... Essentially, you now have AI talking to AI..
What good are you for? Since the AI is so smart it technically does not need you.


 Theres a statistic online that said AI generated material has surpassed the human amount of information written online, which i find incredibly worrisome. In my work day i receive and look at so much AI generated material its actually effort to read and most of the time i don't even bother.  And i know im not alone in this. 

Again, what use are you as a human?
